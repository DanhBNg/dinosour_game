# Free exploration pilot
Approved scope: T-Rex and loggerhead turtle only. Dedicated /animal/:id/explore route from a white controller button on action screen. Two deterministic low-poly Three.js environments, 120-unit playable diameter, fixed-level movement, WASD/arrows and touch D-pad, follow camera, obstacle and boundary collision. Models and original animations reused; no Blender, missions, combat, or other species in this pilot.

Implementation: roam-world.js builds/disposes scenery and resolves horizontal collision. free-roam.js owns separate renderer/model/action lifecycle, inputs, damping and camera. app.js integrates route/back/load cancellation. Narration dots removed without removing measurement labels/range graphics. Verify keyboard/touch movement and release, diagonal normalization, boundary, route reload/back, desktop/mobile rendering, and browser errors.
